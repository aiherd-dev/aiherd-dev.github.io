AIHerd Privacy Policy

This policy covers the AIHerd Android companion app and the AIHerd services it uses. AIHerd connects your phone to coding sessions on computers you choose.

Information used by the app

AIHerd stores connection settings and credentials on your device. Connection credentials are protected using Android's encrypted preferences. The app exchanges terminal output, commands, session information, and other content you request with your connected computer. Your computer and the coding tools you run have their own storage and data practices.

We use technical usage and connection logs to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the service. These can include request times, app versions, connection results, and network metadata. Hosting and network providers may process IP addresses and request metadata to deliver and secure the service.

Diagnostics you choose to send

If you send a connection report, AIHerd uploads the diagnostic logs, app version, and platform to our support service. Logs can contain host names, network addresses, device details, and error messages. The app attempts to remove credentials from logs, but you should check logs before sharing them. Our report store does not add your source IP address to the saved report; source addresses are used temporarily for rate limiting.

Diagnostic reports are eligible for deletion after 30 days and are removed during subsequent report uploads. A support notification containing the report identifier, app version, receipt time, and log size may be sent through Telegram. It does not include the uploaded log text.

Push notifications and service providers

AIHerd uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. Firebase processes app-instance or installation identifiers, notification registration information, and technical metadata needed to provide its service. A notification registration address is also sent to your paired computer. Notification content passes through the notification service when notifications are sent. See Firebase's privacy information.

AIHerd uses hosting and network providers to deliver services, and Google Play processes app purchases under Google's own terms and privacy policy. AIHerd does not receive your payment-card details through the Android app. We do not use an advertising SDK in the app.

Retention and your choices

You can remove saved connections in the app or clear the app's storage to remove locally saved app data. Data on your computer must be removed there. You can control notification permission in Android settings and choose whether to send a diagnostic report. Operational logs for our intake services are configured to expire after 90 days, with deletion applied as log files rotate; storage limits may remove them earlier. Older entries in shared host logs and records held by hosting or network providers follow their respective retention settings and are not covered by that limit. Relay address records use a default retention period of 90 days after their last activity.

To ask about privacy or request deletion of a diagnostic report, contact support@aiherd.dev and include the report receipt identifier if available. Do not send passwords or connection secrets in your request. Third-party service providers manage their own records under their applicable policies.

Security and changes

All data transmitted to AIHerd-operated services is encrypted in transit. Diagnostic uploads and Firebase communication use HTTPS, and protected connection methods include SSH and encrypted relays. User-configured HTTP connections are intended only for testing within your own infrastructure; that HTTP traffic is not sent to AIHerd-operated services.

We may update this policy as the app and services change. The published policy will show its last updated date.
